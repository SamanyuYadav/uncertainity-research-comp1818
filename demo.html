When confidence fails

Can an AI tell when a digit becomes hard to read?

What to notice

Noise changes the pixels. Dropout repeats the prediction with different hidden units disabled, then averages the probabilities. Calibration adjusts how sharply the probabilities are concentrated.

A high probability can still accompany a wrong answer. In the measured experiment, clean calibration made strong-noise predictions worse, while mixed calibration only partly reduced that harm.

This is the real seed-0 checkpoint. Interactive noise draws differ from the fixed research experiment. These probabilities are not guarantees about any individual image. Full evidence is in the accompanying report.
